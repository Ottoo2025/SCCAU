Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding our clinical mission and community support protocols.

Our mission is to reduce the sickle cell burden in Northern Uganda through genotype screening, genetic counseling, and vocational empowerment for Warriors to ensure they lead healthy, self-reliant, and productive lives.

Transparency is our priority. We maintain a strict pledge where 92% of all funds go directly into field projects like testing kits and empowerment tools, while only 8% supports the essential administrative logistics.
