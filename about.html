Our Genesis

From Local Clinics to a Regional Alliance.

I

It began in the health centers of Akitenino Cell, Lira City West. Our founders saw beyond clinical charts—they witnessed the silent struggles of warriors fighting Sickle Cell Disease across the Lango sub-region.

What started as a commitment to service soon crystallized into a mission. We realized that medical care alone wasn't enough; we needed a grassroots movement that prioritized clinical diagnostics and vocational empowerment.

"We didn't just find a gap in the healthcare system; we found a calling to fill it with hope and clinical excellence."

The SCCAU Leadership Executive Team & Co-Founders

The 5-Year Journey

Our Roadmap to 2030

A strategic plan to scale our impact from Northern Uganda to the entire nation.

2025

Foundation & Launch

Official registration on 04 Nov 2025 in Akitenino Cell, Lira City West, establishing our HQ.

2027

Clinical Integration

Setting up early screening centers and training health workers across the Lango sub-region.

2029

Regional Expansion

Extending outreach to the Acholi sub-region and launching mobile clinic units for remote villages.

2030

National Alliance

Fully established national presence with a unified policy advocacy platform and nationwide survivor networks.
