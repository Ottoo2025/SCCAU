A Message from our Director

Championing Hope in Northern Uganda

"Sickle Cell is not a death sentence; it is a challenge we can overcome through knowledge and unity. We don't just test genotypes—we build a future where every child in Lira and beyond lives an empowered and stigma-free life."

Sickle Cell Champions Alliance Uganda is a not-for-profit organization founded by young, passionate health workers. Registered on 4th November 2025, we address the urgent need for awareness, stronger advocacy, and improved access to care across the Lango and Acholi sub-regions.

Ongol Ivan

Executive Director, SCCAU

Medical Excellence

Our Clinical Intervention

Delivering precision diagnostics and compassionate care to the Sickle Cell community across Lango and Acholi sub-regions.

Diagnostic Screening

Providing high-precision genotype testing through mobile units and door-to-door visits to ensure early identification and informed health choices.

Professional Counseling

Offering expert pre-test and post-test counseling for individuals and families to understand results and manage the psychosocial impacts of SCD.

Warrior Mentorship

Pairing SCD warriors with experienced role models to navigate treatment and social challenges, improving overall quality of life.

Home-Based Care

Extending clinical reach through regular home visits to provide essential medicine, supplements, and direct medical guidance to those in need.

We believe that with strategic partnerships with like-minded organizations, we can scale our impact to reach sickle cell warriors nationally.

From Stigma to Strength: A Champion's Journey

In a small village outside Lira, 8-year-old Aceng used to miss school every month due to severe pain crises. Her family felt helpless until they met the Alliance during a community outreach.

"The Alliance didn't just give my daughter care; they gave her back her childhood. Now she lives without the fear of the next crisis."

— Aceng’s Mother

Aceng is one of over 700 beneficiaries. Your support brings testing and care to the furthest corners of Northern Uganda.

Our Vision

Rooted in Lira: The Roadmap to a Stigma-Free Uganda

Founded in Akitenino Cell, Lira City West, our journey began with a group of passionate health workers dedicated to bridging the awareness gap. While our operations are centered in Northern Uganda, our mission is to eliminate SCD stigma nationwide.

01

Phase 1: Lira City Base

Establishing our core awareness, screening, and peer-mentorship support programs.

02

Phase 2: Lango & Acholi Regions

Scaling mobile screening clinics and door-to-door support to rural districts across the North.

03

Phase 3: National Impact

Influencing policy and healthcare infrastructure to ensure every person with SCD in Uganda lives an empowered life.
